Privacy policy

Vision Quest Games, published by Emrld Labs.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Summary

Vision Quest Games runs entirely offline on your iPhone or iPad and collects no data from children or parents. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no network connections in the app.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. The developer does not collect any data from this app.

What we collect

None. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress, XP, level, unlocked badges, Alchemy Lab discoveries, and high scores stored locally on your device

Network use

Vision Quest Games contains no networking code (no URLSession) and makes no internet connections.

Children

Vision Quest Games is rated 4+ and made for children and families. Because the app collects no personal information, requires no account, contains no links or chat features, and works offline, no data is ever gathered from children. The games are designed for play around visual tracking, color matching, and memory, and are not a medical treatment.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
