Help and support

Common questions about Vision Quest Games.

Frequently asked questions

Is Vision Quest Games completely free?

Yes. Version 1.0.1 is free to download and includes all six games and bonus modes with no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

What ages is Vision Quest Games designed for?

The app is rated 4+ on the App Store and is designed for children ages 4 and up and their families.

Is Vision Quest Games a medical treatment or vision therapy?

No. While the games are designed around visual tracking, color matching, hand-eye coordination, and visual memory, they are for play and entertainment only. They are not a medical treatment, clinical measurement, or substitute for professional eye care.

What games are included?

The collection includes six games plus bonus modes: Space Explorer (with Rocket Dodge), Balloon Adventure (with Keepy Uppy), Memory Master (with Shell Game), Nitro Snail Runner, Treasure Maze (with Match-3), and Alchemy Lab.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. After you download the app from the App Store, all games, music, and progression tracking work completely offline.

Does the app collect any information from children?

No. Vision Quest Games collects no personal data, requires no sign-in or account, and contains no analytics or third-party tracking. Game progress is saved only on your device.

How do I get help or report a bug?

Email hello@emrldlabs.com, or open an issue on GitHub at github.com/workmanl/Vision-Quest-Games/issues.

Contact support

Email: hello@emrldlabs.com

Report a problem or ask a question on GitHub.

When you get in touch, include your device model, the system version and the app version (1.0.1 is the current release) so the problem can be reproduced.
